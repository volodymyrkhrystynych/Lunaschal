Journal link archive

Links you attach to a journal entry are printed to PDF here, in this browser, using its signed-in sessions. Leave this tab open; each page opens briefly in a background tab and closes again.

Chrome shows a “started debugging this browser” bar while a page is being printed — that is this extension reading the page it opened. A page that is a sign-in form or a paywall even here is not printed; the journal shows it as needing a login. Sign in to the site in this browser, then press Retry on the card.
